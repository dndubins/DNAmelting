### DNA Melting Curve Programs
Clarifying the terminology:<p>
Self-complementary, n=1 (Unfolded <-> Folded)<p>
Self-complementary, n=2 (SS1 + SS1 <--> Duplex)<p>
Non-self-complementary, n=2 (SS1 + SS2 <-> Duplex)<p>
Self-complementary, n=3 (3 x SS1 <--> Triplex)<p>
Self-complementary, n=4 (4 x SS1 <--> Quadruplex)<p>

The DSO files in this directory use a fitting routine called Direct Search Optimization - a numerical
technique introduced to me by Dr. Rein Luus, at the Dept. of Chemical Engineering and Applied Chemistry, 
University of Toronto. Great teachers not only change what we can do, they change who we are. He was one 
such teacher.
